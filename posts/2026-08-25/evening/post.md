 ↳ What happened: Generalist AI launched GEN-1.5, a robotics foundation model that learns new physical tasks from a single 3 to 12-second demonstration. It processes video footage directly into real-time motor commands without lengthy training cycles.
 ↳ Why it matters: Zero-shot visual learning drastically cuts the time and data required to deploy adaptive automation in physical environments.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/24/generalist-ai-releases-gen-1-5-a-robot-foundation-model-that-learns-new-tasks-from-one-3-12-second-demo/

🔹 The Risk of AI-Driven Skill Decline in Coding
 ↳ What happened: A widely discussed industry analysis warns that over-reliance on AI coding tools could prevent developers from building deep technical expertise. Offloading core problem-solving to AI tools risks eroding critical debugging and architectural skills over time.
 ↳ Why it matters: Developers need to treat AI as a productivity multiplier rather than a total replacement for fundamental engineering knowledge.
 ↳ Read story: https://larsfaye.com/articles/ai-coding-will-prevent-expertise

My take:
 ↳ As hardware and model speeds accelerate, deep human expertise remains our ultimate safety net. Using AI to build faster is great, but understanding the underlying mechanics will always separate top engineers from the rest.
