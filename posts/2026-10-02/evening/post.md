Today in AI & Tech 🔥
AI infrastructure is hitting a major wall while enterprise agent deployment accelerates.

🔹 Power approval set to delay Oracle's Wisconsin AI datacenter
 ↳ What happened: Oracle's massive new AI data center in Wisconsin is facing significant delays due to pending power approvals. As energy demands for machine learning infrastructure skyrocket, local grid capacities are struggling to keep up with the sheer scale of modern computing requirements.
 ↳ Why it matters: Cloud and DevOps engineers must factor prolonged grid approval timelines and local power constraints into future infrastructure deployment roadmaps.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theregister.com/on-prem/2026/10/02/power-approval-set-to-delay-oracles-wisconsin-ai-datacenter/5300832

🔹 OpenAI’s Dot agent is enterprise software that can also order your dinner
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI has rolled out a new agent platform called Dots, designed to handle complex enterprise tasks while also managing personal workflows like ordering food. Unlike abstract assistants, Dots acts as a practical companion capable of executing multi-step digital actions.
 ↳ Why it matters: As consumer and enterprise boundaries blur in agentic software, engineering teams need to build secure, context-aware API permissions.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1004096/openai-chatgpt-dots-hands-on-agent

🔹 Microsoft MAI Voice Models Arrive in LiveKit for Expressive TTS Agents
 ↳ What happened: Microsoft's expressive AI voice models are now officially integrated into LiveKit Agents via a new text-to-speech plugin. This gives developers a standardized and documented way to build highly responsive, natural-sounding voice applications.
 ↳ Why it matters: Integrating low-latency, expressive speech generation makes real-time conversational AI agents much more practical for customer-facing production apps.
 ↳ Read story: https://dev.to/alifar/microsoft-mai-voice-models-arrive-in-livekit-for-expressive-tts-agents-2gg7
