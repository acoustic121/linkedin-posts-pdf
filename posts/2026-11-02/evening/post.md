Today in AI & Tech 🔥 Massive funding rounds, open scientific models, and clever grid solutions are shaping the future of infrastructure.

🔹 Anthropic reveals $900B AI spending commitments ahead of IPO
 ↳ What happened: Ahead of its upcoming public offering, financial disclosures reveal that Anthropic has locked in massive spending commitments totaling $900 billion for AI infrastructure. This unprecedented capital outlay highlights the staggering cost of scaling next-generation language models.
 ↳ Why it matters: Expect continued high demand for enterprise cloud resources and compute infrastructure as foundational model builders race to scale.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.ft.com/content/4d91dbab-6fe4-45ab-beee-d77e091cb37f

🔹 Deep Principle Releases Hydrogen-1.0: A 753B Open-Weight Model for Scientific Discovery
 ↳ What happened: Deep Principle has launched Hydrogen-1.0, a massive 753-billion parameter open-weight model specifically trained for chemistry, materials science, and life sciences. Built by post-training Z.ai's GLM-5.3 on laboratory records, it aims to accelerate complex scientific research.
 ↳ Why it matters: Open-weight scientific models give researchers and specialized engineering teams powerful localized tools without relying entirely on closed APIs.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/02/deep-principle-hydrogen-1-0-open-weight-scientific-model/

🔹 OpenAI pitches data center power plants as a way to protect nearby areas
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI announced its new 'Grid Protectors Program,' starting with a pilot data center in Georgia that promises to free up to 1,000 megawatts of power for local communities. The initiative aims to address massive energy grid strains caused by rapidly expanding AI data centers.
 ↳ Why it matters: Infrastructure and cloud operations teams will increasingly need to factor local power grid partnerships and sustainability into large-scale deployments.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1024604/openai-data-center-grid-protection-program
