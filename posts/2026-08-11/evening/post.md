Today in AI & Tech 🔥 From tiny 14MB AI models running on edge devices to big streaming platforms putting boundaries on synthetic music, here is what is happening in tech today.

🔹 Spotify Limits Recommendations for 'AI Personas'
 ↳ What happened: Spotify will soon label synthetic AI artists and filter them out of automated user recommendations starting mid-September. The platform will add an "AI Persona" tag to artist profiles to ensure human creators stay front and center.
 ↳ Why it matters: Expect content platforms to establish strict metadata tags and filtering APIs as synthetic media scales up.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/entertainment/977815/spotify-ai-persona-label-recommendations

🔹 Anthropic Rolls Out Invisible C2PA Watermarks for Claude
 ↳ What happened: Anthropic is embedding invisible, machine-readable watermarks into text and images generated by Claude. This system follows C2PA standards to meet upcoming AI transparency regulations in Europe and help identify synthetic content across the web.
 ↳ Why it matters: Building C2PA provenance and verification into AI apps will soon be a standard requirement for enterprise deployment.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/977823/anthropic-claude-ai-watermarks-c2pa-text-images

🔹 Needle2: A 14MB Agentic LLM for Edge Devices
 ↳ What happened: Cactus released Needle2, an extremely compact 14MB AI model designed for tool calling, structured extraction, and agentic workflows on local hardware. It runs on smartphones, wearables, smart home devices, and small robotics without requiring cloud connections.
 ↳ Why it matters: Local micro-LLMs enable low-latency, privacy-first agentic automation on edge devices without heavy cloud compute costs.
 ↳ Read story: https://cactuscompute.com/needle

My take:
 ↳ As AI models grow smaller and more accessible, the industry's focus is shifting toward content provenance and platform curation to protect human creators and maintain data authenticity.
