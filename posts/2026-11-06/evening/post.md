Today in AI & Tech 🔥
Big shifts in cloud computing deals, autonomous work agents, and the hidden infrastructure costs of AI data scraping.

🔹 Microsoft Winds Down Oracle AI Cloud Capacity Deal
 ↳ What happened: Microsoft is scaling back its deal to rent AI cloud computing capacity from Oracle as it expands its own data center infrastructure. The move signals a shift from emergency capacity leasing toward internal infrastructure self-reliance for large-scale AI workloads.
 ↳ Why it matters: Cloud engineers should prepare for shifting workload placements as tech giants consolidate AI hosting into proprietary data centers.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.reuters.com/business/microsoft-is-winding-down-oracle-deal-rent-ai-cloud-capacity-bloomberg-news-2026-11-06/

🔹 Perplexity Launches AI "Teammate" Agents for Work
 ↳ What happened: Perplexity released new AI agents designed to handle complex, multi-step ongoing work alongside human teams. These agents can manage background research, execute scheduled tasks, and proactively share updates within enterprise workflows.
 ↳ Why it matters: Developers can start shifting routine research and monitoring tasks to autonomous agents rather than managing manual prompt cycles.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.perplexity.ai/en-GB/hub/blog/teammates-are-agents-for-complex-ongoing-work

🔹 AI Data-Scraping Bots Overwhelm University Libraries
 ↳ What happened: University libraries globally are experiencing server outages caused by aggressive AI training bots scraping public research archives. The surge in automated traffic is crashing academic servers and forcing institutions to restrict open public access.
 ↳ Why it matters: Rate limiting, edge firewall filtering, and bot mitigation are becoming urgent infrastructure priorities for any public data host.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/university-libraries-ai-data-scraping-bots-9.7408287

My take:
 ↳ As AI models demand vastly more data and compute, infrastructure reliability—from web traffic management to multi-cloud strategy—is quickly becoming the ultimate bottleneck in tech.
