🔹 Google Gemini Notebook Connects with E-Books
 ↳ What happened: Google's AI note-taking app, Gemini Notebook, now allows users to query purchased titles directly from Google Play Books. The new Expert Intelligence feature lets users talk to long-form books as structured knowledge sources.
 ↳ Why it matters: It streamlines deep research by turning static published books into interactive, queryable databases without manual copying.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/tech/985567/google-gemini-notebook-expert-sources-books

🔹 Graph Memory Overcomes Vector Search Limits
 ↳ What happened: Standard vector search struggles when AI agents need to recall complex relationships across long conversations. Integrating knowledge graphs with vector databases helps agents retain context and prevent memory loss over time.
 ↳ Why it matters: Combining graph structures with semantic search leads to significantly more accurate, context-aware enterprise AI agents.
 ↳ Read story: https://dev.to/aws/graph-memory-when-vector-search-fails-2aeh
