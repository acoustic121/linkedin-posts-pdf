Today in AI & Tech 🔥
Big shifts in coding tools, enterprise AI integrations, and smart device strategy.

🔹 GitHub Launches Copilot Fusion Generally Available
 ↳ What happened: GitHub released Copilot Fusion, allowing developers to set effort levels while AI dynamically picks the best underlying model for each task. It introduces transparent task-based pricing to help teams optimize LLM costs against performance.
 ↳ Why it matters: Engineering teams can now automate model routing based on task complexity without manually toggling between different AI vendors.
 ↳ Read story: https://dev.to/kuponau/github-copilot-fusion-vs-claude-code-which-is-cheaper-per-task-oct-2026-4hee

🔹 OpenAI & Oracle Partner to Bring Enterprise Apps to ChatGPT
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI and Oracle announced an expanded partnership to embed Oracle enterprise business applications and AI agents directly into ChatGPT. The move allows corporate users to query internal databases and trigger workflows straight from the chat interface.
 ↳ Why it matters: Enterprise cloud architecture is moving toward conversational interfaces as the unified control plane for back-office systems.
 ↳ Read story: https://openai.com/index/oracle-apps-in-chatgpt

🔹 Microsoft Retires Copilot AI Assistant on Smart TVs
 ↳ What happened: Nearly two years after partnering with LG and Samsung to bring Copilot to smart TVs, Microsoft is officially ending support for the feature. The decision marks a pivot away from living-room AI interfaces toward core productivity environments.
 ↳ Why it matters: Voice and chat AI assistants need clear, high-intent utility to justify maintenance overhead on consumer IoT hardware.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/tech/1021143/microsoft-lg-samsung-copilot-ai-assistant-smart-tvs-ending

My take:
 ↳ AI deployment is maturing from speculative everywhere-all-at-once experiments to focused, high-value integrations in enterprise workflows and developer tools.
