Today in AI & Tech 🔥
Massive LLMs running on tiny RAM, 3x faster inference speedups, and major EU copyright shifts!

🔹 Running a 284B-Parameter LLM on 3.2GB RAM
 ↳ What happened: A developer ran DeepSeek-V4-Flash, a massive 284B-parameter model, on a basic laptop using pure C code and smart memory mapping. Despite the model needing 160GB on disk, clever system engineering kept peak memory usage to just 3.23GB.
 ↳ Why it matters: It proves resource-efficient local AI execution is possible without expensive cloud GPU infrastructure.
 ↳ Read story: https://dev.to/ronak_parmar_033c50d168b5/i-ran-a-284b-parameter-llm-from-32gb-of-ram-in-plain-c-cbp

🔹 Liquid AI Unveils LFM2.5-DSpark Draft Models
 ↳ What happened: Liquid AI released lightweight 300M-parameter draft models designed for speculative decoding. This technique speeds up token generation by up to 3.18x while producing identical model outputs.
 ↳ Why it matters: Developers can dramatically reduce inference latency and cloud compute costs without sacrificing output quality.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/20/liquid-ai-releases-lfm2-5-dspark-draft-models-that-deliver-up-to-3-18x-faster-decoding/

🔹 EU Reaffirms AI-Generated Content Lacks Copyright Protection
 ↳ What happened: Official legal clarifications confirm that content generated purely by AI models cannot be copyrighted in the EU. Human creative involvement remains mandatory for intellectual property protection.
 ↳ Why it matters: Teams building commercial products in Europe must incorporate human curation to safeguard their intellectual property.
 ↳ Read story: https://mathstodon.xyz/@maxpool/117128107757895678

My take:
 ↳ We are moving rapidly toward hyper-efficient model execution at both the system level and the server level. As compute bottlenecks clear up, legal and intellectual property frameworks will become the next major boundary for enterprise AI adoption.
