🔹 Anthropic Begins Trading on Wall Street

🔹 Gemini on Mac Gains Local Computer Use
 ↳ What happened: Google updated its Gemini desktop app for macOS with computer use capabilities, enabling it to navigate apps, inspect files, and execute multi-step workflows. Users can now delegate repetitive cross-application tasks directly to the model on their workstations.
 ↳ Why it matters: Agentic AI is moving off the browser and straight into OS-level orchestration, redefining how engineers automate daily desktop workflows.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/tech/1037902/google-gemini-macos-ai-computer-use

🔹 OpenAI Reveals the "Performance Factory" Behind ChatGPT
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI detailed how it uses autonomous Codex-powered workflows to proactively identify, diagnose, and resolve production latency bottlenecks across its serving infrastructure. The setup creates a closed-loop system where models optimize the code running models at hyperscale.
 ↳ Why it matters: Self-healing infrastructure and agent-driven performance engineering are quickly becoming essential patterns for high-throughput cloud operations.
 ↳ Read story: https://openai.com/index/how-openai-performance-factory-keeps-chatgpt-fast
