Today in AI & Tech 🔥
From enterprise cloud billing to AI infrastructure controls, here are the top 3 stories shaping engineering today.

🔹 ChatGPT Enterprise Launches on AWS Marketplace
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI has officially listed ChatGPT Enterprise on the AWS Marketplace. This allows organizations to purchase enterprise AI features using their existing AWS accounts, billing structures, and committed cloud budgets.
 ↳ Why it matters: Streamlining cloud billing removes procurement friction and makes enterprise AI adoption much faster for DevOps teams.
 ↳ Read story: https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-enterprise-aws-marketplace

🔹 The Unexpected Risk of a 100% AI Coding Mandate
 ↳ What happened: A year after an organization mandated fully AI-generated code, engineering teams report severe tech debt and a total loss of codebase comprehension. Developers are struggling to maintain or debug complex features they didn't manually design.
 ↳ Why it matters: Auto-generating code without human understanding creates massive long-term operational risks for software teams.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeCode/comments/1xq2n3t/a_year_after_the_100_ai_mandate_nobody/

🔹 OpenAI Unveils "Control Room" for Infrastructure Workloads
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI announced Control Room, a central system built to track model training and inference workloads across their compute fleets. It gives teams real-time visibility and the ability to instantly kill jobs if safety or security anomalies occur.
 ↳ Why it matters: Emergency kill-switches and centralized telemetry are becoming essential requirements for managing high-scale AI infrastructure.
 ↳ Read story: https://openai.com/index/control-room

My take:
 ↳ AI tooling is advancing faster than our operational governance. Successful engineering teams won't just generate code faster—they will invest heavily in infrastructure controls to keep systems maintainable and secure.
