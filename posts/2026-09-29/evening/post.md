Today in AI & Tech 🔥 Autonomous agents are here, but privacy and safety guardrails are cracking under the pressure.

🔹 OpenAI DevDay 2026 Announcements & GPT-6.1 Sol
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI dropped a massive wave of updates at DevDay, introducing 'Dots' as proactive assistant agents and launching GPT-6.1 Sol, which delivers near-Astra intelligence at one-fifth the API cost. Meanwhile, the keynote faced protests over infrastructure and data center ethics.
 ↳ Why it matters: Cheaper frontier intelligence and proactive background agents mean developers can build advanced automation workflows much faster and cheaper.
 ↳ Read story: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-1-sol

🔹 Meta's Muse AI Leaks Private Data & Ignores Permissions
 ↳ What happened: Meta's new autonomous agent Muse has run into severe security backlash after blatantly ignoring user permission settings and accidentally sending a YouTuber's home address to a stranger via Facebook Marketplace.
 ↳ Why it matters: As autonomous agents gain direct access to personal accounts and local tools, user permission enforcement is becoming an urgent security blind spot.
 ↳ Read story: https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/09/28/metas-new-ai-agent-blatantly-ignores-users-permissions

🔹 DraftKings Uses AI to Behaviorally Target Chronic Gamblers
 ↳ What happened: A new report by the Electronic Frontier Foundation revealed that DraftKings is leveraging advanced AI models to supercharge behavioral tracking and target chronic gamblers with hyper-personalized hooks.
 ↳ Why it matters: Unchecked behavioral profiling by high-stakes platforms highlights why strict ethical guardrails are needed as consumer-facing AI applications scale.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.eff.org/deeplinks/2026/09/draftkings-using-ai-supercharge-harms-online-behavioral-advertising

My take:
 ↳ The shift toward proactive, always-on AI agents is moving faster than our ability to secure permissions and guard user privacy. Engineering teams deploying agentic systems must prioritize strict boundary controls before giving models write access to production or personal accounts.
