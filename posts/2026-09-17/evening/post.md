🔹 Neon just gave AI agents their own database, no signup required. Here's how the handoff actually works.
 ↳ What happened: Neon has introduced a seamless way to provision Postgres databases instantly for coding agents without requiring traditional signups or manual configurations. This solves a major workflow bottleneck where autonomous agents grind to a halt when they finally need a real database instance.
 ↳ Why it matters: Eliminating manual database setup lets coding agents work truly autonomously from schema creation to deployment.

🔹 Stanford Researchers Release Paper2Agent: Turning Research Papers Into AI Agents That Reproduce Results and Run on New Data
 ↳ What happened: Stanford researchers have published Paper2Agent, a tool that automatically converts academic research papers into validated AI agents. These agents can successfully reproduce experimental results and process new data sets with high accuracy.
 ↳ Why it matters: It dramatically accelerates how fast new machine learning research can be tested and applied in real-world products.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/16/stanford-researchers-release-paper2agent-turning-research-papers-into-ai-agents-that-reproduce-results-and-run-on-new-data/
