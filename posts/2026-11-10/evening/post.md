🔹 Fireworks AI Breach Impacts Notion
 ↳ What happened: AI provider Fireworks AI suffered a security incident that impacted third-party services including Notion. The breach highlighted vulnerabilities in relying on external AI APIs for production workloads. Notion issued an advisory outlining mitigation steps.
 ↳ Why it matters: External AI APIs expand your attack surface, making third-party vendor vetting essential for cloud ops.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.notion.com/help/security-incident-involving-a-third-party-ai-vendor

🔹 AI Usage Generates Fragile Codebases
 ↳ What happened: A new analysis shows how heavy reliance on AI coding tools creates fragile software over time. While AI speeds up code creation, it lacks architectural context and introduces subtle bugs. Developers pasting code without thorough reviews are accumulating heavy technical debt.
 ↳ Why it matters: AI boosts velocity, but unvetted AI code will dramatically increase your future maintenance costs.
 ↳ Read story: https://evanxmerz.com/post/ai-usage-generates-a-fragile-codebase/

🔹 CoreWeave Bond Yields Top 16% as AI Debt Rises
 ↳ What happened: Specialized GPU cloud provider CoreWeave saw its bond yields surge past 16% due to growing credit stress. The company took on massive debt to build GPU data centers, but investors are questioning near-term AI returns.
 ↳ Why it matters: Vendor debt stress in GPU cloud providers could soon impact hosting costs and compute availability.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/06/coreweave-bond-yield-tops-16percent-as-ai-credit-stress-rises.html
