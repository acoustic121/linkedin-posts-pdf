Today in AI & Tech 🔥
Apple eyes a server comeback, Google opens smart homes to AI agents, and Anthropic expands into workplace tools!

🔹 Apple Eyes Server Comeback for AI
 ↳ What happened: Apple is reportedly planning to re-enter the server hardware market, possibly partnering with NVIDIA. After retiring its Xserve line in 2011, Apple is now looking to build specialized server infrastructure to handle the massive compute demands of AI.
 ↳ Why it matters: Infrastructure demands are forcing tech giants back into custom hardware design to control costs and performance.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/tech/996321/apple-servers-ai-nvidia

🔹 Google Home Opens to Any AI Agent via MCP
 ↳ What happened: Google is opening its smart home ecosystem to third-party AI agents like Claude using the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Agents can now analyze home data, review camera feeds, and control connected devices directly.
 ↳ Why it matters: MCP is fast becoming the standard for connecting AI agents directly to real-world hardware and APIs.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/tech/996310/google-home-mcp-integration-agentic-ai-smart-home-price-release-date

🔹 Anthropic Launches Claude Docs and Slides
 ↳ What happened: Anthropic introduced Docs and Slides directly inside Claude, allowing users to create, edit, and export presentations and documents within chat. This puts Claude in direct competition with Google Workspace tools.
 ↳ Why it matters: AI chatbots are rapidly transforming into complete office workspace suites.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/996234/anthropic-one-claude-cowork-docs-slides

My take:
 ↳ Standardized protocols like MCP are accelerating how AI interacts with physical systems and software ecosystems. As hardware providers scale specialized compute and software layers gain action capabilities, AI is moving from text responses to real infrastructure execution.
