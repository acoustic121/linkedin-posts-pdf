🔹 Banks are trying to offload AI data center debt
 ↳ What happened: Financial institutions that underwrote massive loans for the AI data center boom are now actively trying to offload that debt amid market selloffs and rising interest rates. The infrastructure rush is facing its first major reality check on Wall Street.
 ↳ Why it matters: Slower capital availability could mean tighter budgets for scaling cloud infrastructure and hardware purchases.
 ↳ Read story: https://startupfortune.com/banks-are-now-trying-to-offload-the-ai-data-center-debt-that-just-underwrote/

🔹 Welcome Muse Spark Open by Meta
 ↳ What happened: Meta has dropped Muse Spark Open on Hugging Face, introducing a brand new open-weight frontier multimodal mixture-of-experts model to the developer community. This brings high-performance multimodal capabilities directly into open-source hands.
 ↳ Why it matters: Engineers now have powerful new open alternatives for building multimodal pipelines without relying entirely on closed APIs.
 ↳ Read story: https://huggingface.co/blog/meta-muse-spark-open

🔹 Your MCP Server is the attack surface now
 ↳ What happened: Security discussions on Dev.to highlight that Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers are rapidly becoming primary targets for attackers. As AI agents connect to local tools and databases, these integration layers present critical new entry points.
 ↳ Why it matters: Securing your MCP endpoints and permission boundaries is now just as important as protecting traditional API gateways.
 ↳ Read story: https://dev.to/relayshield/your-mcp-server-is-the-attack-surface-now-1em8
