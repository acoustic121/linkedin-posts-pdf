Today in AI & Tech 🔥
From ultra-efficient open MoE models to desktop applications for AI agents, here is what’s shifting in tech today.

🔹 Aleph Alpha Drops Kolibri 78.1B MoE Model
 ↳ What happened: Aleph Alpha released Kolibri, an open-weight English-German model with 78.1 billion total parameters. Despite its size, it only activates 3.46 billion parameters per token, drastically lowering resource costs while supporting a massive 1 million token context window.
 ↳ Why it matters: Sparse MoE architectures are making long-context models cheaper and faster to run without sacrificing output quality.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/10/04/aleph-alpha-releases-kolibri-a-78-1b-open-weight-english-german-moe-model-with-only-3-46b-active-parameters/

🔹 DeepSeek Harness v0.2 Brings Native Desktop Apps
 ↳ What happened: DeepSeek updated its open-source agent framework with native desktop apps for macOS and Windows. The v0.2 release adds a plugin manager, file and code-change review features, and scheduled background task runs.
 ↳ Why it matters: Native desktop apps make local AI agent management and workflow automation far more practical for software developers.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/10/03/deepseek-harness-v0-2-brings-official-desktop-apps-to-its-open-source-agent-harness/

🔹 Google Upgrades Gboard with Verifiable Privacy
 ↳ What happened: Google Research moved its federated learning compute into hardware-secured Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs). The architecture logs access policies publicly, giving Gboard externally verifiable differential privacy during training.
 ↳ Why it matters: Combining confidential hardware with public audit logs sets a brand-new standard for privacy-preserving AI training.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/10/04/google-research-moves-federated-learning-into-tees-gboard-now-trains-with-externally-verifiable-differential-privacy/

My take:
 ↳ Efficiency and verifiable privacy are driving today's AI engineering updates—whether that means activating just 3.5B parameters out of 78B or proving data protection directly in hardware.
