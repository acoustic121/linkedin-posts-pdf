Today in AI & Tech 🔥
From autonomous agents handling payments to local AI milestones, here are the top developments you need to know today.

🔹 OpenAI Introduces ChatGPT Wallet
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI has officially rolled out ChatGPT Wallet, providing a secure method for AI agents in ChatGPT Work, Codex, and other tools to complete purchases with user approval and existing card protections.
 ↳ Why it matters: As AI agents transition from assistants to active participants in commerce, secure financial integration becomes a critical infrastructure layer for developers.
 ↳ Read story: https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-wallet

🔹 Google Announces Gemini 4 Hydrogen Model
 ↳ What happened: Google's new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model is designed to drastically improve comprehension of complex inquiries, including prompts featuring multiple parts, heavy typos, or highly specific constraints.
 ↳ Why it matters: Better handling of complex, multi-layered queries means fewer prompt workarounds and more reliable natural language interfaces for enterprise apps.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1032505/google-search-ai-mode-gemini-4-hydrogen

🔹 llama.cpp v1.0.0 Released
 ↳ What happened: The ggml-org team has officially dropped version 1.0.0 of llama.cpp, establishing a much stronger and more standardized foundation for running large language models locally.
 ↳ Why it matters: A robust local execution framework reduces cloud dependency, giving operations and engineering teams more cost-effective ways to run private AI workloads.
 ↳ Read story: https://huggingface.co/blog/ggml-org/llama-cpp-v1

My take:
 ↳ We are moving fast from passive chat interfaces to active agents that handle money and run locally—meaning system architecture and security boundaries need to evolve just as quickly.
