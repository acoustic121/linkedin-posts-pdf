Today in AI & Tech 🔥
From massive cloud infrastructure breakthroughs to AI tools built directly into your chat apps, here is your daily tech breakdown!

🔹 OpenAI Details Scaling Storage to 1 Billion Users
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI revealed how it scaled its backend storage engine, Habitat, from a simple Python library into a globally distributed storage platform. The system now seamlessly serves over 1 billion active ChatGPT users while processing up to 22 million requests per second.
 ↳ Why it matters: Managing stateful distributed storage under unprecedented AI workloads is fast becoming the ultimate test for modern cloud architecture.
 ↳ Read story: https://openai.com/index/scaling-storage-one-billion-users-part-one

🔹 Slack Adds Vibe-Coding for Interactive Charts and Dashboards
 ↳ What happened: Slack unveiled Slackforce Surfaces, a feature allowing users to build interactive reports, live polls, presentations, and dashboards directly within chat channels. Users can simply describe what they need using natural language to build functional tools on the fly.
 ↳ Why it matters: Embedding real-time low-code execution into workspace channels eliminates context switching for engineering and operational teams.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/tech/989853/slackforce-surfaces-launch

🔹 Meta Updates AI Prompts Following Privacy Concerns
 ↳ What happened: Meta is updating its AI chatbot prompt suggestions after a viral video showed the system asking invasive questions regarding a user's young children. The company responded by revising its default prompt engine to avoid soliciting overly sensitive personal data.
 ↳ Why it matters: AI safety guardrails must govern not just how chatbots answer questions, but also how they proactively prompt users for input.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/tech/993974/meta-ai-prompt-invasive-suggestions

My take:
 ↳ Building shiny AI features is the easy part—scaling backend infrastructure and maintaining strict consumer privacy guardrails are the real operational challenges. As engineers, mastering resilient data pipelines and proactive safety loops will define the next era of successful tech deployment.
