 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/tech/991809/amazon-prime-video-ai-lip-sync-dubbing

🔹 Why AI Agent Loops Fail (And How to Fix Them)
 ↳ What happened: A new breakdown highlights that most agent failures are loop failures, not model failures. When agents loop through Think-Act-Observe without state control, they get stuck repeating tasks endlessly.
 ↳ Why it matters: Reliable AI system engineering requires treating agent loops like resilient distributed state machines rather than relying solely on prompt tweaks.
 ↳ Read story: https://dev.to/hosseinhezami/the-agent-loop-nobody-talks-about-think-act-observe-repeat-34m8
