🔹 Amazon Blocks Meta's Muse AI Agent
 ↳ What happened: Amazon has officially blocked Meta's new Muse AI agent from accessing amazon.com on behalf of users, deploying pop-ups citing unauthorized scraping and automated shopping attempts. This marks a major escalation in the battle between retail giants and AI platforms over autonomous web interaction.
 ↳ Why it matters: As consumer-facing AI agents become more prevalent, platform access controls and bot mitigation will become a massive bottleneck for cross-platform automation.
