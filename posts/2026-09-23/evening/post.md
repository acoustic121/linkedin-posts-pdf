🔹 Better prompt caching for GPT-6
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI has rolled out major upgrades to prompt caching for GPT-6, featuring higher cache hit rates, explicit breakpoints, and advanced diagnostics. These controls are designed to drastically lower latency and slash API token costs for heavy production workloads.
 ↳ Why it matters: Lower latency and reduced inference costs make large-scale LLM deployment significantly more viable for production cloud applications.
 ↳ Read story: https://openai.com/index/better-prompt-caching-for-gpt-6

 ↳ What happened: Sen. Bernie Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar have introduced aggressive new legislation to outright ban the development of artificial superintelligence. The bill targets systems defined as having uncontrollable autonomous capabilities and proposes severe criminal penalties for violators.
 ↳ Why it matters: Regulatory scrutiny on high-end AI research is intensifying, which will eventually shape compliance frameworks for enterprise AI infrastructure.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/999443/bernie-sanders-ai-superintelligence-ban-act
