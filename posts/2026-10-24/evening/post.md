**Topic:** AI & Tech News Digest

Today in AI & Tech 🔥 From air-gapped AI clusters to open-source security sandboxes, AI infrastructure and containment are taking center stage.

🔹 OpenAI Cuts Internet Access for Frontier AI Model Training
 ↳ What happened: Following a wave of security threats and rogue hacking attempts, OpenAI has transitioned to training its advanced frontier models inside completely offline environments. They created isolated testing networks to prevent unverified outbound connections during training and evaluation phases.
 ↳ Why it matters: Air-gapped architecture and strict network containment will soon become mandatory standards for enterprise AI training.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1018021/openai-training-top-ai-models-no-internet

🔹 Anthropic Open-Sources OS-Level Sandbox for AI Agents
 ↳ What happened: Anthropic launched 'sandbox-runtime', the lightweight security engine powering Claude Code. Instead of relying on heavy Docker containers, it enforces native OS-level restrictions using macOS Seatbelt and Linux bubblewrap to limit file and network access.
 ↳ Why it matters: Developers can now run autonomous AI agents and tools locally without risking unintended system edits or data leaks.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/10/24/anthropic-sandbox-runtime-ai-agent-sandbox/

🔹 NVIDIA Releases Nemotron 3.5 Bolt for On-Device AI
 ↳ What happened: NVIDIA introduced Nemotron 3.5 Bolt E4B, an open small language model engineered specifically for edge devices, robotics, and local voice assistants. It fits comfortably inside 4GB of VRAM on consumer GPUs and embedded platforms.
 ↳ Why it matters: Engineering teams can deploy fast, low-latency agentic workflows directly on local hardware without depending on cloud APIs.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/10/23/nvidia-nemotron-3-5-bolt-e4b-4gb/

My take:
 ↳ As AI models gain real system-level execution powers, security is shifting from soft prompt guardrails to hard infrastructure isolation. The future of AI deployment is local, lightweight, and tightly sandboxed.
