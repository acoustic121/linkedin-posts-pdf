Automation is scaling faster than our security and attention spans can handle.

🔹 1Password lets AI agents use your passwords without seeing them
 ↳ What happened: 1Password introduced a feature enabling AI agents to log into user accounts securely without ever viewing or handling the actual passwords. This prevents bots from gaining free access while still automating sign-in workflows.
 ↳ Why it matters: It bridges the gap between agentic automation and credential safety for daily workflows.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1016399/1password-ai-agent-password-sign-in

🔹 PHP: The three little letters wreaking havoc across the web
 ↳ What happened: Two newly discovered vulnerabilities in PHP—which powers up to 70 percent of websites globally, including Wikipedia—have put massive swaths of the internet at risk. The security flaws have caused widespread disruptions and downtime for web administrators.
 ↳ Why it matters: Core web infrastructure remains fragile, highlighting the need for rigorous dependency patching.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/tech/1016574/php2shell-security-vulnerability-websites-down

🔹 AI Can Parallelize the Work. It Cannot Parallelize My Attention
 ↳ What happened: A developer using AI coding agents realized that while coding assistants could spawn multiple windows of active work, human attention could not keep up. The author ultimately had to roll back a month of work after failing to properly supervise the automated output.
 ↳ Why it matters: AI multiplies code velocity, but code review and engineering oversight remain human bottlenecks.
 ↳ Read story: https://dev.to/pietro_palmesi_b8b68ba0f6/ai-can-parallelize-the-work-it-cannot-parallelize-my-attention-ag5
