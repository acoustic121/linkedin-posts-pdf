Today in AI & Tech 🔥 Quick rollbacks, invisible AI watermarks, and autonomous big rigs hit the road!

🔹 Instant Rollbacks for AI Prompts
 ↳ What happened: A major hurdle in AI operations is that fixing a bad prompt usually requires re-building and deploying an entire application artifact, causing prolonged outages. Decoupling prompts from application code allows them to be managed dynamically like cloud config.
 ↳ Why it matters: Treating prompts as dynamic cloud infrastructure instead of hardcoded application code enables sub-minute rollbacks and prevents costly downtime.
 ↳ Read story: https://dev.to/multigrid/rolling-back-a-bad-prompt-deploy-in-under-a-minute-35fh

🔹 Google Gemini Lets Users Remove Visible AI Watermarks
 ↳ What happened: Google is updating Gemini to allow users to toggle off visible watermarks on AI-generated images, videos, and music. While visible logos can now be hidden for cleaner creative assets, backend tracking like SynthID remains active.
 ↳ Why it matters: Developers and creators gain production-ready media assets without sacrificing behind-the-scenes authenticity tracking.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/tech/980416/google-gemini-ai-watermarks-removal

🔹 Self-Driving Trucks Hit California Highways
 ↳ What happened: Autonomous vehicle startups Aurora Innovation and Kodiak AI received official permits from the California DMV to begin testing heavy-duty self-driving trucks on public highways. This marks a major regulatory shift for autonomous freight logistics.
 ↳ Why it matters: Long-haul autonomous trucking is moving from controlled test tracks directly into enterprise supply chain infrastructure.
 ↳ Read story: https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/14/self-driving-trucks-are-officially-testing-on-california-highways/
