Today in AI & Tech 🔥
AI tools might help complete homework faster, but they won't pass your exams. Here are the top 3 tech stories you need to know today!

🔹 AI Boosts Homework Scores, But Tanks Exam Performance
 ↳ What happened: A new study revealed that while students using AI tools saw a huge spike in their homework grades, their performance on unassisted exams dropped significantly. Researchers found that relying on AI created a false sense of mastery without building real core problem-solving skills.
 ↳ Why it matters: Relying heavily on AI generation without understanding underlying concepts weakens foundational engineering and analytical skills.
 ↳ Read story: https://canews24.online/?p=71

🔹 Ranking the Top GPU Neoclouds of 2026
 ↳ What happened: A new industry report compared major GPU neocloud providers like CoreWeave, Nebius, Lambda, Crusoe, and Groq based on published pricing and compute capacity. The analysis highlights how specialized cloud providers are challenging traditional hyperscalers with tailored AI hardware setups.
 ↳ Why it matters: Infrastructure engineers can optimize AI training and inference costs by leveraging specialized neoclouds over traditional cloud providers.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/08/21/best-gpu-neoclouds-2026/

🔹 Over 1 Million Users Click LinkedIn’s "AI Slop" Button
 ↳ What happened: LinkedIn recently introduced a feature letting users flag generic or low-quality AI-generated messages and content. Over one million people have already used the button, signalling widespread user fatigue over automated outbound outreach.
 ↳ Why it matters: Automated AI communications require genuine human editing to avoid immediate rejection by users and algorithmic moderation.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/983502/linkedin-ai-slop-button-one-million-people-message

My take:
 ↳ As AI becomes integrated into every workflow, real comprehension and quality matter far more than sheer output volume. Whether building cloud architecture or writing code, human insight remains the ultimate differentiator.
