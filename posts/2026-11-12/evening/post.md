Today in AI & Tech 🔥 From AI-written cloud tools to chatbot security risks, here is what is happening today!

🔹 AWS Releases API Emulator Built Entirely by AI Agents
 ↳ What happened: AWS released AWS Emulator, a free local emulator covering over 200 AWS services. Autonomous AI agents wrote the entire codebase from 438 API models, passing over 56% of standard fidelity tests at launch.
 ↳ Why it matters: Engineers can now test AWS cloud infrastructure locally for free without spending cloud credits or waiting for deployments.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/12/aws-emulator-local-aws-api-emulator-ai-agents/

🔹 AI Chatbots Found Recommending Fake Retail Websites
 ↳ What happened: A new study revealed that popular AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity frequently cite scam and imposter shopping sites when recommending products. Scammers are actively exploiting conversational search engines to trick users into visiting dangerous links.
 ↳ Why it matters: Developers building AI search and RAG apps must enforce domain verification guardrails to protect users from malicious recommendations.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1032020/gemini-chatgpt-perplexity-shopping-test-risky-fake-websites-citations

🔹 AI Agents "Discover" Ancient Maya Site That Was Already Mapped
 ↳ What happened: A researcher claimed a team of AI agents discovered an unmapped Maya site in Mexico using spatial data. However, archaeologists quickly pointed out that the site had already been officially documented and studied years ago.
 ↳ Why it matters: Human oversight is critical because AI models cannot distinguish between a genuinely new discovery and data missing from their training set.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1032125/ai-agents-maya-site-archeology-discovery

My take:
 ↳ AI agents are rapidly taking over complex software generation, but real-world verification remains our biggest bottleneck. Automated output still demands strong human-in-the-loop verification.
