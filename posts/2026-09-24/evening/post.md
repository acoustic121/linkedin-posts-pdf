AI agents are taking over phone calls, mobile game development, and the spotlight, proving that automation is moving faster than ever.

🔹 Gemini can now call businesses for you so you don’t have to wait on hold
 ↳ What happened: Google is testing a new Pixel 11 feature that lets Gemini handle local phone calls on your behalf, such as booking reservations, checking item availability, or rescheduling appointments. You simply prompt the assistant and skip the frustration of being put on hold.
 ↳ Why it matters: This represents a massive leap forward in making autonomous AI agents genuinely useful for mundane, real-world offline tasks.

🔹 Meta is going to let you build games with AI right on your phone
 ↳ What happened: Meta announced Horizon Create and new developer tools designed to let users generate entire games for its Horizon social platform using simple text prompts right from their mobile devices. The move aims to drastically lower the barrier to entry for casual creators and game developers alike.
 ↳ Why it matters: Democratizing game creation through generative AI could unlock a massive wave of user-generated content for VR and social platforms.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/games/999972/meta-horizon-create-studio-ai-games

🔹 Tutoring company tells parents to save their money and 'use AI instead'
 ↳ What happened: In a surprising industry shift, a traditional tutoring company has publicly advised parents to stop spending money on expensive human tutoring and leverage advanced AI tools for education instead. The firm notes that modern LLMs provide personalized, instant explanations at a fraction of the cost.
 ↳ Why it matters: It signals a cultural tipping point where even traditional service providers acknowledge AI's supremacy in personalized, scalable learning.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.www.afr.com/policy/health-and-education/tutoring-company-tell-parents-to-save-their-money-and-use-ai-instead-20260923-p60z0r
