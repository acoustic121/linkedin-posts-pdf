Today in AI & Tech 🔥
AI is moving fast from standard chat interfaces straight into automated security defense, real-time banking, and low-power cloud hardware.

🔹 OpenAI Releases GPT-6 Cyber for Safe Security Research
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Cyber, a specialized model designed to detect software vulnerabilities and suggest code fixes. Unlike prior releases, it is trained specifically to stay within authorized scopes and prevent unauthorized system exploits.
 ↳ Why it matters: Security and DevOps engineers gain a guardrailed automated assistant for proactive patching without risking rogue exploitation.
 ↳ Read story: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-cyber

🔹 Anthropic's Claude AI Connects Directly to Bank Accounts
 ↳ What happened: Anthropic launched a Personal Finance feature that lets Claude connect directly to bank and credit card accounts using Plaid. Users can now analyze spending habits, automate budget tracking, and query financial data in natural language.
 ↳ Why it matters: It shifts AI assistants from simple conversation tools into functional personal administrative agents handling live, sensitive data.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1027652/anthropic-claude-plaid-personal-finance

🔹 Qualcomm Announces Cloud AI 100 Ultra Card for Low-Power Inference
 ↳ What happened: Qualcomm unveiled its Cloud AI 100 Ultra, a 150W data center accelerator packed with 128GB memory at 548GB/s bandwidth. The hardware focuses on maximizing AI inference efficiency per watt in enterprise infrastructure.
 ↳ Why it matters: Cloud operations engineers can significantly lower power budgets while scaling high-throughput inference workloads.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.qualcomm.com/data-center/products/cloud-ai-100-ultra
