🔹 Scaling Database Storage for 1B ChatGPT Users
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI revealed how they scaled their storage architecture to serve over a billion users. By using smart caching, multi-tenancy, and Azure Cosmos DB, they achieved 12x faster data read speeds globally.
 ↳ Why it matters: High-performing AI applications depend heavily on robust database caching and efficient storage architectures to keep latency low.
 ↳ Read story: https://openai.com/index/scaling-storage-one-billion-users-part-two

🔹 AI Carbon Footprints: Texas vs. Sweden
 ↳ What happened: A new benchmark showed that running the exact same AI query emits 8.5 times more CO2 in Texas than in Sweden. The massive gap comes down to regional energy grids and clean power availability.
 ↳ Why it matters: Cloud deployment strategies must start accounting for regional carbon intensity alongside cost and latency.
 ↳ Read story: https://berget.ai/en/blog/per-request-co2-press-release

🔹 AI Builds Fully Cited Wiki from 45,000 Enron Emails
 ↳ What happened: Developers created a structured wiki generated entirely from 45,000 raw Enron emails. Every single fact presented in the wiki cites its exact original source email for total verifiability.
 ↳ Why it matters: Automated knowledge extraction with strict citations shows how enterprises can turn messy internal data into accurate, auditable documentation.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.thisandthat.chat/enron/

My take:
