Today in AI & Tech 🔥
AI tools are moving from isolated chat windows directly into daily workspace files, enterprise workflows, and developer terminals.

🔹 Google Gemini Adds Native File Generation and Editing
 ↳ What happened: Google updated Gemini to let users generate, preview, and edit files directly inside the chat window. The update supports PDFs, Google Docs, Sheets, slides, Markdown, and code without needing to switch tools.
 ↳ Why it matters: Native editing inside the chat interface eliminates context switching and streamlines developer documentation workflows.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1022519/google-gemini-file-editing-documents-pdfs

🔹 OpenAI and ServiceNow Expand Enterprise Partnership
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI and ServiceNow are deepening their integration to embed AI models directly into enterprise operational workflows. ChatGPT and Codex will also be able to perform actions inside ServiceNow systems automatically.
 ↳ Why it matters: Enterprise AI is shifting from Q&A search tools to active agents capable of executing IT and business operations.
 ↳ Read story: https://openai.com/index/openai-servicenow-partnership-expansion

🔹 Tackling Context Window Bloat in Claude Code
 ↳ What happened: Tool calls and MCP integrations often dump megabytes of raw logs and snapshots into AI context windows, driving up costs and degrading response quality. Developers are adopting dedicated context pruning setup routines to condense data before prompting models.
 ↳ Why it matters: Managing data hygiene before it hits the prompt is becoming as important as model selection for cost and performance.
 ↳ Read story: https://dev.to/dedupedai/stop-claude-code-context-bloat-with-context-mode-a-5-step-setup-l26

My take:
 ↳ We are entering an execution-focused phase of AI where deep system integration matters more than raw model size. The platforms that win will be those that integrate seamlessly into real-world editing and operational workflows.
