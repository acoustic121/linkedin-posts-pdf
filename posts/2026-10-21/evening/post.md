🔹 Specializing SLM Vocabularies Cuts Inference Costs by 77%
 ↳ What happened: Researchers demonstrated that tailoring small language model (SLM) vocabularies to specific domain datasets drastically reduces token output size and compute overhead. In real-world benchmarks, this strategy sliced inference costs by up to 77%.
 ↳ Why it matters: Domain-specific vocabulary optimization is becoming a powerful lever for cloud teams looking to scale LLM workloads while keeping infrastructure budgets in check.
 ↳ Read story: https://huggingface.co/blog/Dharma-AI/vocabx-vocabulary-specialization
