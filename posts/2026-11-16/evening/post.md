Today in AI & Tech 🔥 Massive data center power demands and AI security threats are making infrastructure efficiency and defense top priorities.

🔹 US Data Centers Power Surge Reaches Country-Scale Levels
 ↳ What happened: A study published in Nature Sustainability reveals US hyperscale data centers now consume more electricity than entire mid-sized nations. Fossil fuels remain the primary energy source powering this compute surge driven by AI workloads.
 ↳ Why it matters: Sustainable power capacity and workload efficiency are becoming critical operational bottlenecks for cloud infrastructure.
 ↳ Read story: https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1034668/data-centers-electricity-climate-pollution-study-harvard

🔹 FBI Issues Alert Over Artex AI Hacking Tool
 ↳ What happened: The FBI warned that attackers are using Artex AI, an open-source tool, to breach corporate networks in under three hours. The tool automates complex penetration steps, allowing attackers to exploit vulnerabilities faster than teams can patch them.
 ↳ Why it matters: AI is drastically shrinking attack windows, making automated patch management and continuous security monitoring mandatory.
 ↳ Read story: https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/16/fbi-says-artex-ai-open-source-hacking-tool-used-to-breach-networks-in-under-three-hours/

🔹 OpenAI Expands Free Model Access to 200,000 Scientists
 ↳ What happened: OpenAI is expanding its ChatGPT for Academic Researchers program worldwide. The initiative grants 200,000 scientific researchers free access to frontier models to accelerate daily workflows and scientific discoveries.
 ↳ Why it matters: Giving researchers direct access to advanced AI accelerates cross-disciplinary research and establishes new standards for scientific tools.
 ↳ Read story: https://openai.com/index/expanding-chatgpt-for-academic-researchers

My take:
 ↳ As AI models drive power consumption sky-high while enabling faster automated cyberattacks, energy efficiency and automated security are becoming our top engineering priorities.
